daydream village

see how two actions can change the fate of a village...


welcome to daydream village! in the village, you get two actions every day in order to make everyone in the town live a perfect day! with good actions, you can earn coin multipliers, and those can earn you loot boxes (still under development pls do not try otherwise game may crash). pls try to make this town a happy place, or else bad things will happen...
