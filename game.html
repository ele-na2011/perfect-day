I Game Guide

two actions a day to improve the happiness of a village and earn coins! each action you take will affect the happiness of the village. drag and drop from the backpack to the field to place items. you can also click on an item in the backpack to place it in the field. once you have placed two items, the day will end and you will see how your actions affected the village. each item increases or decreases the happiness of the village by a certain amount. try to make the village as happy as possible! double right click on an item in the field to remove it.
